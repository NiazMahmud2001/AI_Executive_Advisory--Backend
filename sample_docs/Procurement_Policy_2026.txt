Oasis Peak Contracting LLC: Procurement Policy 2026
Effective 1 February 2026 · Owner: Finance Department

Section 1: Scope
This policy applies to all purchases of goods and services by any department.

Section 2: Approval limits
Up to AED 10,000: Department Head.
AED 10,001 to AED 100,000: Finance Manager.
Above AED 100,000: CFO and CEO jointly.

Section 3: Quotations
Purchases above AED 25,000 require three written quotations from different suppliers.

Section 4: Split purchases
Splitting a purchase into smaller orders to stay under an approval limit is not allowed.

Section 5: Payment terms
Standard supplier payment terms are 60 days from the invoice date.
